# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss technical decisions without personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers can remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed.
For a confidential conduct report, use the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/statespace-tech/PostgreOS/security/advisories/new)
and start the title with `Conduct report`.

This policy applies to project spaces and project-related communication. The project can
adopt a longer community standard as the contributor community grows.
